Reset Your Windows Password Without Losing Any Data

A safe, reliable method using the built-in Windows Recovery Environment. Follow every step carefully to regain access to your PC.

📋 28 Steps 🛡️ Data Safe ⏱️ ~15 Minutes
⚠️

Important Before You Start

This method modifies system files inside System32. Follow each step exactly as shown. Do not skip any step. This guide is intended for recovering access to your own computer.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these 28 steps carefully

1

Go to the Login Screen

Start your PC and wait until the Login Screen appears.

2

Click on the Power Icon

Click on the Power icon at the bottom-right corner of the screen.

3

Shift + Restart

Press and hold the Shift key, then click on Restart.

4

Keep Holding Shift

Do not release the Shift key after the blue screen appears.

5

Choose an Option → Troubleshoot

A blue screen appears with the title Choose an option. Click on Troubleshoot.

6

Advanced Options

Click on Advanced options.

7

See More Advanced Options

Click on See more advanced options.

8

System Image Recovery

Click on System Image Recovery.

9

Cancel the Warning

If a screen appears showing “Windows cannot find a system image on this computer” (or similar), click Cancel to close it.

10

Click Next

After the previous screen, a new window appears — click Next.

11

Click Advanced

Click on Advanced.

12

Install a Driver

Click on Install a driver.

13

Select This PC

Select This PC.

14

Open Local Disk (C:)

Click on the Local Disk (C:) drive.

15

Open the Windows Folder

Click on the Windows folder.

16

Open System32

Find the System32 folder and click on it.

17

Rename utilman → utilman12

Find utilman (application) and rename it to utilman12.

18

Find cmd

After renaming, find cmd (Command Prompt) in the same folder.

19

Rename cmd → utilman

Rename cmd to utilman.

20

Back to Choose an Option

Go back to the Choose an option screen.

21

Click Continue

Click on Continue.

22

Please Wait

Wait for the Please wait screen to finish loading.

23

Login Screen Appears

After waiting, the Login Screen appears.

24

Click the Accessibility Icon

Click on the man icon (Accessibility / Ease of Access) in the bottom-right corner of the screen.

25

Run the Command

It opens Command Prompt. Type the command control userpasswords2 and press Enter.

26

Reset Password

Select your user name and click on Reset Password.

27

Leave Password Blank

It shows Create New Password — leave it blank and click on OK.

28

Close & Sign In

Close Command Prompt and click on Sign in / Login.

✓

Congratulations!

Your Windows password has been successfully reset without losing any kind of data.

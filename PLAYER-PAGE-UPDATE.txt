NPLcode Player Page Update

- Match detail page now sends each watch-link click to player.html.
- player.html plays direct HLS (.m3u8), MP4/WebM/Ogg, and DASH (.mpd) video URLs in a video element.
- Other HTTP(S) URLs are treated as provider embed pages and displayed in a fullscreen-capable iframe.

Important limitation:
A generic third-party webpage cannot be converted into a video-only stream from browser JavaScript. The provider must supply a direct media URL or a video-only embed URL. Same-origin policy prevents NPLcode from inspecting/extracting video elements from unrelated websites. DRM-protected streams require the provider's authorized player/license flow.

Deploy all project files to the same Vercel project. Keep player.html in the same directory as detail.html.

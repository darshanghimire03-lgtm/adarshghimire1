Who I Am

About Me

I'm Darshan, a developer based in Nepal with a passion for crafting high-quality Android applications and modern web experiences. I work across the full stack, from frontend interfaces to backend services, but my heart lies in mobile development.

My work spans social platforms, school and college companion apps, games, utility tools, and responsive websites. I primarily build with Kotlin for Android and JavaScript/TypeScript for the web, often backed by Firebase and other cloud services. I care about clean architecture, polished UI, and shipping products that deliver real value.

I'm deeply invested in building for Nepal — creating tools that serve local communities, from Bikram Sambat calendar systems to Nepali-language platforms and rental utilities. Whether it's an app or a website, I aim to make technology accessible and useful for people here first.

Who I Am

About Me

I'm Darshan, an independent Android developer based in Nepal. I build apps entirely on-device — no PC required — using AndroidIDE as my primary development environment.

My work spans social platforms, school and college companion apps, games, and utility tools, most of them built with Kotlin and backed by Firebase. I care about clean architecture, polished UI, and shipping things that actually work in the hands of real users.

I have a strong pull toward building for Nepal specifically — Nepali-language tools, Bikram Sambat calendar systems, and community platforms that serve people here first.
